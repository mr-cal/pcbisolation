I was unsatisfied with the brake and turn signal combos available online, so I made my own. It has running lights, brake
lights, turn signals, and license plate illumination. I used high output LEDs and used the extra space on the PCB for
heat sinking.

If you're curious about the bike, it's a 1983 Kawasaki CSR 305. Here's [a picture of it](motorcycle-brake-and-turn-light-04.jpg) next to the
Mississippi River.

More details about the brake light [here](/blog/motorcycle-brake-turn-light/).
