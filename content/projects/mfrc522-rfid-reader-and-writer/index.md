This RFID board uses NXP's MFRC522 RFID chip. It's an excellent chip, but the only development boards currently
available are poor quality and poor design (see example [here](mfrc522-rfid-reader-and-writer-02.jpg)).

I designed this as a high quality alternative. It uses a much larger board and more components, but the increase in
read/write range is phenomenal! You can find my design files
[here](http://circuitmaker.com/Projects/Details/John-Kovacs-2/MFRC522RFIDboard).
