This guide is for removing keys off a Lenovo ThinkPad T450s and should also work for the T430, T440, T450, and T460.

There are fragile retaining clips on the underside of each key. Repetitive removal will surely break them. Once the clip
breaks, it must be replaced.

## Key Removal

[![lenovo t450s key replace 4](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-04.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-04.jpg>)

Wedge your finger underneath the upper left and pull directly up until you hear a single click.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 5](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-05.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-05.jpg>)

Now wedge your finger underneath the upper right and pull directly up until you hear another single click.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 6](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-06.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-06.jpg>)

[![lenovo t450s key replace 7](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-07.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-07.jpg>)

The key should now be free to be removed.

If you're just replacing the key or removing debris underneath the key, then you are done. If the plastic bracket is
broken, follow the steps below to remove the bracket.

## Plastic Bracket Removal

[![lenovo t450s key replace 8](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-08.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-08.jpg>)

Using a small flathead screwdriver or razor blade, gently push forwards on the little arm. Once you have pushed forwards
enough, you can then push upwards, freeing the arm from its retaining hinge.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 9](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-09.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-09.jpg>)

The arm has been pushed upwards. You can see it is now free.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 10](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-10.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-10.jpg>)

We'll repeat the same procedure on the other side. The second side always requires less force.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 11](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-11.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-11.jpg>)

And it is free. The lower part of the bracket slips out on its own.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 12](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-12.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-12.jpg>)

Continue to use your screwdriver or screwdriver to wiggle the bracket free.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 13](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-13.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-13.jpg>)

And the bracket has been freed!

Reassembly follows the same steps in reverse.

[![lenovo t450s key replace 14](lenovo-t450s-key-replace-14.jpg)](<lenovo-t450s-key-replace-14.jpg>)
